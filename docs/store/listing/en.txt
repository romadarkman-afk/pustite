# title
Let Me In
# short
Night, 1894. Someone knocks, and there is room for few. Who do you let in?
# full
1894. A county village at the edge of the forest. At night no one survives outside, and there are fewer shelters than people.

Someone stands at your door and begs to be let in. You decide. Vampires walk among the villagers, and they don't even know each other.

DAY
Talk to the villagers, check their words against the clues, and read your diary: who said what, who slept where, who promised to open the door. The gathering votes on who to exile. Get it wrong and an innocent person walks into the forest.

NIGHT
Someone knocks. You can look the visitor over (twice a night) and decide whether to open. Bolt, candle, charm, lantern oil: every item has a price, and a charm can crack by morning. The Mimic knocks in the voice of someone you know.

CAMPAIGN
12 chapters in four acts: the village, the mill by the river, the monastery, the station. Summer, autumn, winter and spring, each act with its own weather and its own newcomers. The dead do not come back: their houses stay empty and the living remember them. The ending of each act depends on who lives to see the morning.
